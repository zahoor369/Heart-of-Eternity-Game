# Controls and how to play

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Mouse | Look / orbit the camera |
| Left Shift | Sprint. Faster than any sentinel, but sentinels can hear it |
| Space | Jump |
| E | Use: doors, rune pedestals, secret walls, the Heart of Eternity |
| Q | Echo Lens (14 second cooldown): reveals hidden carvings, points an arrow to the goal, stuns sentinels within 15 m |
| F | Lantern on / off |
| M | Open / close the temple map |
| Esc | Close the map, or pause / resume |

## Goal
1. Find the **Sun Key** (the vault floor is covered in spike tiles; they glow red just before they strike).
2. Open the **Sun Door** and wake the four **runes** in the order shown by the hidden mural (use Q to see it). A wrong rune gives a shock.
3. Cross the **Trap Gallery** and take the **Moon Key**.
4. Open the **Moon Door** and take the **Heart of Eternity**.
5. Follow the golden light back to the entrance before the timer ends. A boulder chases you.

## Optional goals
Five Sun relics (two are behind secret walls: use Q at a dead end and press E on the glowing glyph), life orbs that heal, and a flawless-run bonus.

## Player name and scores
Type your name on the main menu (saved automatically). **High Scores** shows your statistics and the best five runs.
Points: key 200, relic 150, rune puzzle 300, each mission step 150, secret 100, sentinel stunned 25, Heart 500, life orb 10; on escape a bonus for the time left, for health and for a flawless run; dying costs 100.
